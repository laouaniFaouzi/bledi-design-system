```jsx
<Badge tone="verified" icon={<Icon name="shield-check" size={13} />}>Trajet vérifié Bledi</Badge>
<Badge tone="onDark" caps>Europe ↔ Maghreb</Badge>
```

`verified` (gold) is reserved for Bledi-issued trust statements. Everything factual (transport mode, category) is `neutral`.
